Features

Everything you need. Nothing in your way.

From a quick tidy-up to finding one document among thousands, EasyExplorer keeps it simple.

Folder tree, file list and a live preview side by side. Resize the panes or hide them to suit your screen.

Powerful search

Search

Find it in seconds, see it instantly

Type a few letters and results appear as you type, with the folder each one lives in. Tap a result to preview it, then swipe to flip through the rest.

  • Wildcards like *.pdf, and ! to leave things out
  • Filter by type, size, created or modified date
  • Search one folder, a whole drive or every drive at once
  • Cut, copy, delete or jump to the folder straight from the results
Advanced Search

Look inside your documents

Remember a word but not the file name? Advanced Search reads inside documents, PDFs, spreadsheets and presentations, and can even find text in pictures.

  • Combine name, text, type, size and dates in one search
  • Pick exactly which drives to search
  • Skips system files and media to stay fast
ZIP and security

Pack files up and lock them down

Select a few files, tap Zip, and choose whether to protect the archive with a password. EasyExplorer uses strong AES-256 encryption that other zip apps understand.

  • Extract ZIPs with one tap, password-protected ones too
  • Password entered twice, so typos never lock you out
  • Nothing is uploaded: everything happens on your phone

FAQ

Questions, answered

Is EasyExplorer free?

Yes. Every feature is free to use. A few small, non-personalised banner ads keep it that way, and there are never any video or pop-up ads.

Why does it ask for "All files access"?

A file manager needs to see your folders to browse, search, move and delete files on your behalf. Android grants this through the "All files access" setting. EasyExplorer only uses it to show and manage your files on your phone.

Does it upload or collect my files?

No. Browsing, searching, previews and ZIPs all happen on your device. There are no accounts and no analytics. Files leave your phone only when you choose to share them.

I deleted something by mistake. Can I get it back?

Usually, yes. Deleted files go to the Recycle bin (under Tools) and stay there for 30 days, so you can restore them. Undo also reverses your most recent changes.

Does it work with SD cards and USB drives?

Yes. Internal storage, SD cards and USB drives all appear in the folder tree, and you can search one drive, several, or all of them.

Will a password-protected ZIP open on my computer?

Yes. EasyExplorer uses standard AES-256 ZIP encryption, which opens in 7-Zip, WinRAR and most other zip apps. Keep the password safe, because it can't be recovered.
